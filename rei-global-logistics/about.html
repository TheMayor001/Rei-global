About Rei Global Logistics

Committed to dependable customs clearing and freight logistics in Mombasa.

Company Overview

Rei Global Logistics is a premium clearing and forwarding company based in Changamwe, Mombasa. We support importers and exporters with accurate documentation, customs compliance, and seamless cargo movement through the Port of Mombasa.

Our team combines industry expertise with a hands-on approach to ensure timely clearance, transparent communication, and reliable delivery across Kenya.
