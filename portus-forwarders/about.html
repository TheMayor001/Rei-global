About Portus Forwarders

Dependable customs clearance, freight forwarding, and logistics support for local and international clients.

Company Overview

Portus Forwarders is a Kenya-based clearing and forwarding company specializing in customs clearance, freight forwarding, and logistics solutions. We provide reliable, efficient, and professional cargo handling services for both local and international clients.

We simplify the movement of goods by offering end-to-end logistics support, from port clearance to final delivery, backed by strong industry knowledge and a customer-centered approach.

Mission

To provide dependable, efficient, and transparent clearing and forwarding services while building long-term partnerships through professionalism and integrity.

Vision

To become a trusted regional logistics partner recognized for excellence, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

Core Values

Our operations are guided by accountability, service quality, and continuous improvement.

Integrity & Transparency

Clear communication and honest handling at every stage of the shipment process.

Reliability

Consistent service delivery with focus on timely cargo clearance and transport.

Customer Satisfaction

Responsive support and practical solutions built around client needs.

Continuous Improvement

Professionalism and process refinement that strengthen service quality over time.

Management

Portus Forwarders is managed by professionals with hands-on experience in clearing, forwarding, and logistics operations, ensuring efficient service delivery and accountability.

Health, Safety & Compliance

We operate in compliance with Kenya Revenue Authority, Kenya Ports Authority, and relevant regulatory bodies while upholding safety standards and proper cargo handling at every stage.
